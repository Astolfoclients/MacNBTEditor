# MacNBTEditor 🌲

A lightweight, dedicated macOS desktop application designed to view, edit, and patch Minecraft singleplayer world data files (`level.dat`). 

Tired of using clunky online tools or complicated command-line programs just to fix a world setting, this app gives you a simple, clean visual dashboard to modify your game saves instantly on a Mac.

## 🚀 Key Features
* **Universal NBT Editing:** Open any standard Minecraft NBT save file (`level.dat`, `.nbt`, `.dat`) and see everything laid out in a clean, readable text dashboard.
* **Full World Customization:** Change *anything* inside your save world dynamically. Modify seed numbers, rename your world, adjust difficulty settings, toggle game rules, change player inventories, or toggle hardcore mode status.
* **Instant Cheat Activator:** Easily locate the `allowCommands` setting and toggle it from `0` to `1` to instantly turn on cheats/commands in a world where they were disabled.
* **Automatic Formatting & Compression:** You don't have to worry about breaking the file structure. When you hit save, the app automatically reformats, bundles, and safely compresses your changes back into Minecraft's native save file format.

## 🎮 How It Works

1. **Launch the App:** Open the `MacNBTEditor` desktop app from your Applications folder.
2. **Open Your World:** Click the **Open File** button and navigate to your Minecraft saves folder (typically found at `~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/saves/`).
3. **Select `level.dat`:** Load the `level.dat` file of the world you wish to modify. Your entire world settings data structure will instantly fill the screen.
4. **Edit Anything:** Scroll down to find the setting you want to change, click and type your edits directly into the screen.
5. **Save Changes:** Click the **Save Changes** button. The app safely overwrites the file, and you are ready to boot up Minecraft with your modifications live!

## 🔒 Completely Safe & Local
Your game saves never leave your Mac. All decoding, editing, and compression happen entirely locally on your hard drive with zero internet dependencies required.
